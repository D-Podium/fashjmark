Built slowly, on purpose

Every piece starts as a plank of kiln-dried hardwood in our workshop. Joints are cut by hand and glued without screws, so the frame can be repaired instead of replaced.

Finishes are plant-based oils, not lacquer. They wear in rather than wear out, and you can refresh them at home.
